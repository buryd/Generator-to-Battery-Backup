# Generator to Battery Backup

120 V battery backup for the circuits now on a Reliance 310CRK transfer switch. The battery charges from Georgia Power or from the existing Champion generator. 240 V loads stay on the house service panel.

Owner-installed. Labor cost is $0.

## Existing equipment

- Champion open-frame inverter generator, about 7,000 W running / 8,750 W starting on gasoline. Propane output is lower.
- Reliance 310CRK 10-circuit, 30 A manual transfer switch. Those circuits move to the new panel. The switch is not left in series with the inverter.
- Reliance PB30 outdoor L14-30 inlet and the 10 ft generator cord. The inlet lands on the inverter generator port.

## Backup loads

120 V only: two refrigerators, kitchen, living room, and dining room receptacles, two bathrooms, four bedrooms, and the lighting on those circuits. One countertop appliance at a time (microwave, toaster, air fryer, or Keurig). Office desktop with two monitors. Living-room internet gear and a 55-inch TV.

The wall battery is one 14.3 kWh module with a 200 A BMS, so it can supply the 6000XP on its own. Signature Solar listed it at $2,700.00 on October 13, 2026.

Each Reliance toggle picks up a circuit that already has a breaker in the main service panel. Those 15 A and 20 A branch breakers stay. They are not on this buy list. The 60 A two-pole is the one new breaker in the service panel. It feeds the inverter, and it has to match that panel’s brand.

## Connections

- Service-panel 60 A breaker to the inverter grid port only.
- Existing outdoor inlet to the inverter generator port only.
- Inverter load port to the new 120 V panel only.
- Neutrals and grounds stay separated in the new panel.
- The generator inlet does not land in the service panel.

Set generator charging near 2,500 W. One refrigerator on each 120 V leg.

## Before the feeder breaker is turned on

Pull the homeowner electrical permit with the city or county. Georgia allows an owner-occupant to do this work on a house they own and live in (O.C.G.A. § 43-14-13). The inspection still applies.

Send Georgia Power non-export standby form DB 18-23 with the EG4 6000XP spec sheet. The 6000XP does not sell power back to the utility.
